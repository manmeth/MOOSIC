## MOOSIC Song List

MOOSIC is a music streaming application developed to provide users with a personalised and engaging listening experience. The application focuses on making music discovery easier by presenting users with a curated collection of songs that can be explored based on different listening preferences.

The music content for the application was selected to provide variety in terms of artists, genres, languages, moods, and styles. The collection includes both English and Hindi tracks from different artists, giving users a wider range of music to choose from.

The following list contains the songs provided as the music content for the MOOSIC application. The songs are organised into different mixes based on the emotional theme they are associated with.
